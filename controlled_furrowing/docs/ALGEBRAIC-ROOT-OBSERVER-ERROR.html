4. What this closes, and what it leaves open

Previously a small force residual supplied no theorem relating the stored field to an exact stationary point. This calculation closes that link for one frozen finite-quadrature state and transfers the result to its static observer. It is a standard validated-numerics result applied to the project; no new general concept or originality claim follows.

It does not certify the new joint material/gain proposal at all seventeen doses, every compatible material combination, stability, a physical continuation branch, positive body Jacobian between all quadrature points for the unknown root, numerical integration accuracy, continuum spatial error, biological observation semantics or cross-size prediction. The original five-gate objective remains incomplete.
