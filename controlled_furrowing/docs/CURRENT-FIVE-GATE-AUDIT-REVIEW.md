## The new joint candidate is progress, with limits

The independent [435-check direct review](MATERIAL-COHORT-DIRECT-REVIEW.md) binds 17 newly computed nonzero fields and their new-material zero reference. The primary disk/spatial-max stored-field discrepancy decreases from 5.3798356748 to 5.1863872511 µm, an improvement of about 0.1934484237 µm. The disk/regional-mean discrepancy increases from 6.4677162709 to 6.5284885734 µm. Both directions are retained in the current audit.

These comparisons use the same 68 source observations under four observation conventions. They are not 272 independent observations. All four new convention outputs use the same primary material/gain update; the other three separately optimized affine proposals were not directly solved.

The gain changed together with material ratios, and the original gain was not proved nonlinear-optimal. A gain-only optimized control has not isolated a material-ratio benefit. The baseline and new continuation histories also differ; local stationarity and exact stored-map geometry do not establish a unique global branch. The current audit correctly avoids attributing the improvement to a biological material mechanism or claiming improvement under every plausible operator.

## The root certificate closes one link

The author [certificate](ALGEBRAIC-ROOT-CERTIFICATION-PATH.md) and separate [256-bit interval-AD review](ALGEBRAIC-ROOT-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) concern the same archived activation-strength-one, 611-variable baseline field. The latter checks all 9,504 local quadrature terms and proves a root in a normalized coefficient ball of radius \(10^{-11}\), with an independent distance upper bound approximately \(2.625447\times10^{-12}\).

The [actual static-observer application](ALGEBRAIC-ROOT-OBSERVER-ERROR.md) uses the larger of the author and independent distance bounds, includes the exact serialized-node addition gap, and applies the independently reviewed graph-transfer theorem. Its height maximum/mean algebraic-error bound is approximately \(2.437007\times10^{-10}\) µm; the separate apical and basal bounds are approximately \(1.311535\times10^{-10}\) and \(1.125472\times10^{-10}\) µm. These are errors between one exact finite-binary-quadrature root and the stored curves, not experimental precision.

This is a substantive verification advance over merely passing a floating force tolerance. It is nevertheless **one old weak-load field**. It supplies no root certificate for the new 17 material fields, no parameter-cell enclosure, no ideal-quadrature/integral bound, no continuum bound, no energetic stability/global branch theorem and no temporal-source equivalence. Extending a theorem conditionally to a supplied radius does not supply that radius at every other state.

## The next mathematical work can proceed without new author data

The next operation is meaningful and executable: extend the existing validated root/observer calculation to the relevant calibration fields, then to nontrivial parameter cells in an explicitly declared geometry and operator family. For one parameter cell \(C\), a uniform contraction proof would bound

\[
\sup_{\theta\in C}\|R f(u_*;\theta)\|_\infty,
\qquad
\sup_{u\in B_r,\theta\in C}\|I-RD_uf(u;\theta)\|_\infty,
\]

and verify self-mapping, positive domains and observation-transfer conditions throughout the cell. Failure is unresolved unless a separate exclusion proof is available. An existence enclosure on one selected branch does not automatically exhaust all equilibria admitted by a different branch contract.

That calculation can support inner witnesses and validated outer exclusions for a tolerance-indexed compatible-set family. It still needs a coverage argument. The [scale-free material domain](PHYSICAL-PARAMETER-DOMAIN.md) has unbounded ratios near excluded simplex faces. A finite trust box or sensitivity grid does not cover it. A countable compact exhaustion is a representation; finite completion requires a justified tail exclusion or a clearly reported uncovered remainder. Geometry and observation assumptions must be carried with the same quantifiers.

Integration error and continuum finite-element error are separate mathematical tasks. The new root certificate fixes the binary quadrature constants. It does not validate their replacement by ideal Gauss constants or certify the integral/continuum operator. A comparison between meshes is evidence of sensitivity, not a theorem enclosing the continuum solution.

A matched gain-only/path control is another legitimate computation if causal attribution of the local improvement is desired. It is a separate attribution experiment, not a substitute for the full uncertainty-propagated transfer requirement.

## Which questions still require source information?

Public information currently does not fix the exact ROI transport, spatial weights, local-peak averaging, component reference allocation, specimen timing and reporting/censoring rules. The observed zero mean with positive maximum has a precise conditional nonattainment result; it does not license inventing an error allowance or rejecting the biology. Per-record uncropped initial/evolving surfaces and calibration-error information are also needed for a source-matched geometry test and empirically justified discrepancy bounds.

The normalized fluorescence is not a measured mechanical force history. Absolute force-scale calibration would break the proved common-scale symmetry, but it is not required merely to formulate inference of scale-free ratios. Those two issues should not be conflated. Likewise, missing raw-file IDs do not negate the primary source's distinct-sample statement.

An externally measured error law is not necessary for every conditional mathematical result: the family indexed by declared discrepancy allowances remains a valid research object. It is necessary to distinguish that family from an empirically identified biological uncertainty set. Exact acquisition/workflow information would determine which conditional operator and allowances deserve that interpretation.

The prepared data request remains unsent. The paper states that no custom code was generated; a request should seek workflow/settings, masks, registered traces, references and crosswalks, not assert that declared custom code is missing. This review makes no author contact, deployment or live-Space verification claim.
