Independent review of the readiness checkpoint

The checkpoint is consistent with the original contract: the five-gate study remains incomplete. The completed calculations are useful numerical and inference prerequisites. They do not supply a full physical compatible set, revised source-matched prediction envelopes, or a new biological concept. A negative scientific outcome can satisfy an executed requirement; an unperformed operation cannot.

This review compares the unchanged original plan, readiness checkpoint, current gate record, and their actual cited receipts. The review receipt binds the snapshot, current source/input hashes and numerical checks. No fits or protocols were rerun or edited.

Novelty and completion language

The checkpoint correctly rejects novelty for generic nonidentifiability, extra-measurement design, and force-scale symmetry. The bounded transfer audit is now complete as an audit; its conclusion is that exact originality remains unresolved and the proposed witness/gap/measurement result has not been established. The refreshed Gate5 record now states this distinction and cites the new audit and receipt. This resolves the earlier metadata ambiguity without implying completion of a theorem.

There is no obligation to manufacture a successful transfer or an ambiguity pair. An empty compatible set, robustly agreeing predictions, or a failed candidate could be scientifically useful if established under the declared assumptions. The present record appropriately leaves the original objective incomplete until the missing operations are actually performed and verified.
