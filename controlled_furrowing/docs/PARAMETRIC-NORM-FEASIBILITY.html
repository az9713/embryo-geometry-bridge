A concrete energy norm for parameter-cell validation

A norm change alone does not certify useful material cells. This investigation does prove something narrower: the Hessian of the one archived weak-load, finite-quadrature model is positive throughout a displacement-coordinate cube of radius 10−810^{-8}10−8. Preserving the volumetric rank structure also makes a 0.1% gain predictor tube plausible. That tube is still a feasibility result: its complete residual, self-map and directed-rounding gates have not passed here.

The retained receipt separates the initial failed bounds, floating global feasibility screens, and an actual Arb certificate. The read-only script performs no equilibrium optimization and changes no physical model. The target is the original 611-free-coordinate, 12×6, strength-one field used by the root certificate, with μ=1\mu=1μ=1 and finite volumetric coefficient κ=5000\kappa=5000κ=5000. No result here covers the seventeen revised-material fields or an unbounded material domain.

1. What was making the old bound fail

Let H0H_0H0​ be the exact Hessian at the frozen center. The old componentwise estimate first takes absolute values of local Hessian changes, assembles row sums, then multiplies by ∣R∣|R|∣R∣, where RRR approximates H0−1H_0^{-1}H0−1​. This discards cancellations associated with finite-element compatibility and the stiff volumetric term. At nodal radius 10−810^{-8}10−8, the comparable floating bound is 33.5451, so it cannot prove a contraction.

Choose a binary matrix WWW by numerically computing an inverse-transpose Cholesky factor of the archived Hessian. Its numerical construction is not its proof. Thereafter every entry of WWW is treated as an exact binary constant, and define

∥x∥G=∥W−1x∥2,G=W−TW−1. \|x\|_G=\|W^{-1}x\|_2, \qquad G=W^{-T}W^{-1}. ∥x∥G​=∥W−1x∥2​,G=W−TW−1.

The retained WWW is triangular with nonzero diagonal, so this is a genuine norm. Independently assembled Arb matrices verify the stronger useful condition

∥WTH0W−I∥2≤∥WTH0W−I∥∞≤1.312203388023×10−10. \|W^T H_0W-I\|_2 \le \|W^T H_0W-I\|_\infty \le 1.312203388023\times10^{-10}. ∥WTH0​W−I∥2​≤∥WTH0​W−I∥∞​≤1.312203388023×10−10.

The norm inequality applies because the exact transformed matrix is symmetric. Thus positive definiteness is established by interval arithmetic, rather than assumed from a successful floating Cholesky factorization.

3. Why naive energy balls still fail

A metric ball ∥W−1e∥2≤ρ\|W^{-1}e\|_2\le\rho∥W−1e∥2​≤ρ gives local widths

∣(Bqe)i∣≤ρ ∥(BqW)i,:∥2. |(B_qe)_i|\le\rho\,\|(B_qW)_{i,:}\|_2. ∣(Bq​e)i​∣≤ρ∥(Bq​W)i,:​∥2​.

The maximum nodal conversion constant is approximately 14.05555: ∥e∥∞≤14.05555ρ\|e\|_\infty\le14.05555\rho∥e∥∞​≤14.05555ρ. This is a floating diagnostic until its norms are enclosed outward. A metric radius 10−410^{-4}10−4, processed with the same diagonal local majorant, has generalized majorant bound about 120.84. Thus a favorable result on a small nodal cube cannot be promoted to an arbitrary larger energy ball.

Likewise, a 0.1% gain predictor tube of metric radius 10−710^{-7}10−7, treated without volumetric structure, has generalized bound 1.9116 and fails. Those failures are retained.

For the five local bulk variables, write ℓ=log⁡J\ell=\log Jℓ=logJ, v=∇ℓv=\nabla\ellv=∇ℓ, and Q=∇2ℓQ=\nabla^2\ellQ=∇2ℓ. The local bulk Hessian is

That conservative screen fails, even before adding the small center residual and tangent defect. The receipt retains the failure. It identifies a specific next computation: validated subdivision of the one-dimensional remainder integral, or a sharper validated symmetric norm bound. The floating spectral estimates alone would be more favorable, but are not used to declare a cell.

The archived columns named log_* are derivatives at the center. They also equal derivatives with respect to a relative linear coefficient change there. The linear predictor above uses that latter coordinate. A uniform logarithmic parameter box must instead enclose the exponential conversion, not silently identify a finite log change with a relative linear change. The zero apical-tension coordinate is additive and physically one-sided.

Common positive scaling of every force/energy coefficient leaves an unnormalized equilibrium equation unchanged up to a scalar. Fixing μ=1\mu=1μ=1 therefore removes that gauge and leaves dimensionless ratios; it does not remove the potentially large ratio κ/μ\kappa/\muκ/μ, or bound the remaining ratios. An energy-norm calculation at numerical κ=5000\kappa=5000κ=5000 is not a result for an independently measured physiological compressibility and is not a uniform incompressible limit.

7. Actionable outcome

The validated positive-Hessian cube supplies a real local stability prerequisite. The rank-aware inequalities identify a concrete route to parameter-dependent tubes: validate the 0.1% gain case first, including its residual; then add coefficient directions with their exact affine derivatives, all failed boxes retained. A fixed-center nodal box and a parameter-dependent predictor tube are different sets and need separate proofs.

There is no justification for declaring that a finite set of local cells covers the full unbounded material domain. That requires either a defensible compact physical domain or proved asymptotic exclusions and a covering argument. No bounded material prior, continuum error bound, experiment-to-observer identification, cross-size validation, or novelty claim is supplied by this norm analysis.
