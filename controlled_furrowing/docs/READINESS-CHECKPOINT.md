## Candidate next claim, with a falsifiable target

The exact new-claim audit distinguishes a modest result from a generic observation: construct two mechanically admissible models satisfying the **same complete declared small-data contract**, and prove that their larger-region response intervals are disjoint after numerical and allowed observation/geometry errors. A matching extra measurement must separate them at attainable precision. No such pair, rigorous continuum separation, or attainable precision has yet been established here.

Two witnesses would prove a narrow ambiguity counterexample. They would not exhaust the full compatible set required for stronger prediction envelopes. If the declared set is empty, report model/contract incompatibility. If all compatible predictions agree, that is evidence for transfer robustness rather than a reason to manufacture ambiguity. The scientific result determines the conclusion.
