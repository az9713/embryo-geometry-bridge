This proves a better tested joint candidate under one declared operator, not a material-ratio benefit, an observation-robust improvement or a completed compatible set. The full-set and forward-error requirements above remain unfinished.

The observation results also prevent a false shortcut. Static regional-mean identities cannot be imposed on separately peaked temporal summaries. Conversely, a source-reported zero mean with a positive maximum creates nonattainment under a continuous, initially zero, local-peak-average contract, but no positive uniform error floor without additional spatial regularity. The actual acquisition semantics must decide how that mathematical fact applies.
