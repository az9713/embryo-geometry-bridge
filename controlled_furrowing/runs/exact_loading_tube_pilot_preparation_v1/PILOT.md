## Signed continuation test

Write the free-coordinate force as \(F(u,I_K+t)=F(u,I_K)+t(Cu+d)\), with \(C=gA\) and \(d=gb\) the exact active coefficients in the same normalized energy. The independent K spatial proof supplies all 611 signed components of \(q=RF(u_K,I_K)\) at that *same point*. Its small-cube derivative bounds have no role in this larger envelope. On the fresh closed swept box \(Y\), obtain \(Z_c\ge\sup_Y\|\mathrm{Id}-RH(u,I_K)\|_\infty\) and signed row intervals \(D\supseteq R H(Y,I_K)v\), both for the **exact spatial** derivative at fixed K dose. Obtain signed row intervals \(a\supseteq R(Cu_K+d)\), \(k\supseteq RCv\), and \(M\ge\|RC\|_\infty\) from the exact active matrix.

Convexity of \(Y\) and the fundamental theorem of calculus along the displacement segment give

\[
 RF(u_K+tv,I_K+t)\in q+t(D+a)+t^2 k,\qquad 0\le t\le h .
\]

This combines the two linear-in-dose vectors before taking absolute values. Each component range is bounded by its directed lower and upper quadratics, checking the two endpoints and any interior stationary point. Set \(\eta\) to the maximum absolute row bound and \(Z=Z_c+hM\). The exact denominator and strict tests are

\[
 Z<1,\qquad \eta+Zr<r,\qquad e=\eta/(1-Z)<r.
\]

Uniform contraction gives the unique root on each fiber. The translated maps on a fixed corrector cube depend continuously on load, so their uniform contraction yields a continuous root branch. The invertible Hessian and the analytic geometry guard then give a smooth branch. K’s same independently verified \(\operatorname{Sym}(R)>0\) and symmetry of the energy Hessian give a strict minimum by the Lyapunov argument. The normalized curvature floor is \((1-Z)/\|R\|_\infty\). At both endpoints the predictor equals the original exact proof center, so the earlier independently certified posterior balls merely need strict inclusion in the new uniqueness cubes, which the exact plan confirms. That inclusion becomes a same-root conclusion only after the new self-map passes. For future adjacent slabs the test is \(\|p_i(I_*)-p_{i+1}(I_*)\|_\infty+e_i<r_{i+1}\) in either valid direction at the identical dose and operator.
