### Exact certificate

For each sign of \(c\), the optimization maximizes \(c^T d\) over \(d\in B\) and \(\lVert Jd\rVert_\infty\le\epsilon\), where \(B\) is the coordinate box. A rational feasible step gives a lower bound. For nonnegative multipliers \(\lambda^+,\lambda^-\), each feasible step also satisfies

\[
c^Td\le\epsilon\,\mathbf 1^T(\lambda^++\lambda^-)+h_B\!\left(c-J^T(\lambda^+-\lambda^-)\right),
\qquad h_B(v)=\sum_j\max\{v_j\ell_j,v_ju_j\}.
\]

Here \([\ell_j,u_j]\) are the archived box sides. The optimizer proposes steps and multipliers. Rational arithmetic verifies feasibility and computes both bounds. Adding the bounds for \(c\) and \(-c\) encloses the width. The original 68-row primary interval overlaps this replayed 68-row interval. ## Exact symmetries and local sensitivity

The physical energy on the admissible deformation domain is

\[
E[y;I]=\int_\Omega\!\left[\frac\mu2(\lVert F\rVert^2-3-2\log J)+\frac\kappa2(\log J)^2+\frac{gI}{2}\chi_{\rm act}\lVert Fn_0\rVert^2\right]dV
+\sum_{s=a,b}\int_{\Gamma_s}\!\left[\frac{K_s}{2}(J_s-1)^2+T_sJ_s\right]dA.
\]

Here \(F=\nabla y\), \(J=\det F>0\), and \(J_s\) is the surface area stretch. With fixed geometry, activation support, inputs and displacement constraints, and no independent force load, multiplying \((\mu,\kappa,K_a,K_b,T_a,T_b,g)\) by \(\alpha>0\) multiplies \(E,DE,D^2E\) by \(\alpha\). This preserves stationary shapes, their minimum character, and energy ordering. A scale-invariant rule also preserves the selected branch. With \(L_0=33.5\,\mu\mathrm m\), divide \(E\) by \(\mu L_0^3\). The material groups are \(\kappa/\mu\), \(K_s/(\mu L_0)\), \(T_s/(\mu L_0)\), and \(g/\mu\). The geometry contributes separate length ratios. The tangent calculation imposes \(\kappa/\mu=5000\). The physical argument concerns the positive-domain energy, with the numerical trial-inversion penalty excluded. The active term depends on the product \(gI\). If a common unknown dose conversion is introduced by writing \(I=\beta I_{\rm recorded}\), then \((g,\beta)\mapsto(g/a,a\beta)\) for \(a>0\) preserves the entire active term in every specimen. The additional identifiable candidate is the effective ratio \(g\beta/\mu\). The saved five-coordinate calculation takes the recorded dose scale as given. For a regular stationary root, write \(G_i=\partial_u E_i=0\), \(H_i=\partial_uG_i\), and \(B_i=\partial_\theta G_i\). Implicit differentiation gives \(D_\theta u_i=-H_i^{-1}B_i\). A smooth observer has derivative \(D_\theta y_i=-O_{i,u}H_i^{-1}B_i+O_{i,\theta}\). The latter term matters if the observer depends directly on a parameter. For a linearized extension with justified nuisance derivative matrix \(N\), an unrestricted tangent perturbation obeys \(dy=Jd+N\eta\). A scalar target \(c^Td\) is recoverable from this linearized datum exactly when there is a weight vector \(w\) with \(J^Tw=c\) and \(N^Tw=0\). This follows from the row space of \([J\ N]\). Among such witnesses, minimizing \(\lVert w\rVert_1\) minimizes the upper amplification bound for an \(\ell_\infty\) data error. This criterion requires an independently justified nuisance model. 1. **Originality.** Prior work already establishes same-law ambiguity under calibration and discrimination under a new intervention. A particular certified pair and its response under a fixed observer can document an application of that construction. The specimen crosswalk, registered paired profiles and geometry, transported ROI and weights, per-specimen timing and growth reference, component reference allocation, measurement uncertainty, and a matched large \(Q\) measurement remain missing. Even for total shortening, average of temporal maxima and temporal maximum of an average can differ. A common shift of both reference surfaces leaves their difference fixed and changes the component shortenings in opposite directions. A next analytical calculation, after those certificates, would predeclare one admissible local parameter cell and carry an exact spatial force and observer remainder through the 34-channel transfer bound. It would certify the regular root branch and either retain each maximizing location or enclose the allowed switches. Carry geometry, dose or observer nuisance forward only with an explicit scope and source-backed bounds. [Identified-set design](../../docs/VALIDATED-IDENTIFIED-SET.md)

An independent four-root result separates the two large contrast intervals by about 0.154755 µm, with both contrasts negative. This compares model predictions under a shared input. Predictive evaluation needs a frozen calibration and prediction set and a separately matched source outcome with the same shape definition and time/geometry conventions.
