# Exact-zero-only interval polynomial cleanup

The old sparse cleanup used `if coefficient != 0`. Arb comparison returns a certainty result; an undecidable inequality is false. A finite nonzero-width coefficient ball crossing zero can therefore have both `== 0` and `!= 0` false. Discarding it replaces an allowed family of nonzero coefficients by zero and invalidates an enclosure. The constant polynomial with coefficient ball [-1,1] is already a counterexample.

The versioned safe kernel discards a finite Arb coefficient only when `is_zero()` is true. A retained ball crossing zero represents every allowed coefficient. Addition, multiplication, differentiation, composition and finite power expansion then preserve coefficient enclosures by outward arithmetic. Nonfinite coefficients are rejected at cleanup rather than silently removed. Exact Fraction coefficients retain ordinary exact-zero semantics. [FLINT comparison predicates](https://flintlib.org/doc/arb/predicates.html) distinguish exact zero from provable nonzero; the behavior is also directly reproduced in the installed python-flint 0.9.0 runtime. [Python wrapper API](https://python-flint.readthedocs.io/en/latest/arb.html).

`exact_fe_target_safe.py` and `polynomial_jet_safe.py` are new files. Original sources and receipts are retained, with explicit withdrawal metadata. Pointwise Fraction algebra is unchanged; interval families are preserved rather than shortened. The independent safe-kernel review verifies all144 point bulk dictionaries and24 point surface dictionaries exactly, and separately tests zero-crossing arithmetic and interval-field witnesses. Such finite tests complement the arithmetic enclosure argument; they do not replace it.

Every affected complete integration run and its independently coded replay must be repeated under the safe policy before numerical conclusions are restored. Agreement between two implementations that share an unsound primitive is insufficient. Exact-rational moments/tails, old dense-matrix quadrature-root proofs, and root-independent rational geometry certificates are separately scoped and do not become invalid merely by importing a module whose affected path they never execute.

The new point-operator libraries expose computation only. Fresh whole-field wrappers own their immutable protocols and independent prelaunch gates. Their status remains unreviewed until the actual outputs and signed global reductions have been checked. No uniform exact-integral root or novelty follows from this repair alone.
