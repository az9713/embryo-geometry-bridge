## Runtime and bounded reuse

Bulk exact interval moments and finite same-Y corrections dominate, particularly whole-box Hessians and independent replay. Prior four bulk workers took about 31–37 minutes by file timestamps during concurrent runs. This is historical, not a forecast. The remaining producer batch has 4,608 bulk and 768 surface component packets before independent replay. Prioritize cheap metadata, cached variation and geometry before expensive moments. A first wave can exercise one ordinary archived-center pair and one recentered state. Then bound concurrency and preserve complete terminal evidence. If a separate point-only stage is reviewed, a signed force lower bound greater than r already prevents the strict self-map test at that center and radius for any z≥0. It does not exclude roots in the cube.

Reuse common topology, formulas and exact reference-only rule/basis moments only when all operands, chart, indices and precision match. Bind those operands to each case. Cases cannot borrow force/H/corrections or domain bounds from neighbors with the same mesh or source letter. Independently proved point F can carry across radii at exactly the same center, operator and precision with validated tail policy. H can carry to a contained cube under explicit exact inclusion and operator identity. The active energy is affine in exact drive and quadratic in linear strain coordinates. A separately reviewed fixed basis contraction could share that term with matched reference data, but does not share the passive nonlinear terms or license interpolating derivatives.
