# First-wave hull admission policy

The [mathematical hull design](INDEPENDENT-HULL-DESIGN.md) gives the validity argument. The first implementation adds a diagnostic stop when independently constructed and producer intervals are disjoint. Two valid enclosures of the same signed scalar must intersect. A disjoint pair requires the whole case to stay unresolved while the target or numerical construction is checked.

For intersecting intervals, a nesting miss can proceed only after a complete independent enclosure through the same analytic proof. Retain both original intervals, their containment and disjointness outcomes, and the exact hull endpoints. The downstream global proof consumes those hull endpoints. Intersection alone is never evidence of validity. Preserve all prior four proofs and execute no packet work under this policy memo.
