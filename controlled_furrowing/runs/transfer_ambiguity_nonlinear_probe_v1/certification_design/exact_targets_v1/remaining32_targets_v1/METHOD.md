This package reconstructs the original source field and solver cache, checked exact material and drive, independently reviewed binary preconditioner, verification center, auxiliary quadrature rule, structural route maps and exact closed displacement cube for each remaining small field. Each target includes 650 exact coordinate hulls, 611 free coordinates and a zero predictor for its material singleton. The trial box remains a proposed geometry domain. The chart IDs are structural routing metadata. No whole-body embedding, positive-J volume domain, deformed trace bound, cached derivative, force, Hessian, integral, solve or root is asserted by this package.
