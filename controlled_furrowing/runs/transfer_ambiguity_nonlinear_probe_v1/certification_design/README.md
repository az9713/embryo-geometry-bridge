# Fixed-parameter certification for the nonlinear transfer pair

The useful target is a **pointwise finite-element root and observation certificate for each frozen endpoint field**, at its own exact material vector and dose. A five-dimensional tube around the earlier joint candidate would answer a different question. There are 34 nonzero small fields and two large final fields in the target pair. The other eight fields in the 44-checkpoint run document numerical loading. Certifying only the 36 final fields does not certify a continuous loading branch.

## Use the actual observer slack to set the target

Wait for the independent nonlinear readback before setting numerical targets. For each endpoint and small record \(i\), let \(O_{ik}^{\,s}\) be its rigorous **stored-curve** observation interval and \(d_{ik}=\sup_{x\in O_{ik}^{\,s}}|x-y_{ik}|\). With the prespecified conditional tolerance \(\delta\), the remaining margin is \(m_{ik}=\delta-d_{ik}\). If any margin is nonpositive, symmetric inflation of those intervals gives no strict admission margin. Report that outcome. A tighter or shifted complete exact-root observation enclosure could change the answer, but the affine prediction and an arbitrarily small radius cannot be substituted for it. If an actual certificate proves the minimum residual exceeds \(\delta\), that particular enclosed branch point is outside the conditional contract. A solver or enclosure failure alone proves no such exclusion.

Let \(L_0=33.5\,\mu\mathrm m\) and \(\xi_i\) be the maximum dimensionless discrepancy between the stored binary nodal coordinates and the exact rational \(X+\operatorname{inj}u_i\) at the chosen center. If the center is changed, include the center shift in \(\xi_i\) when using the original stored observer, or reconstruct all observables at the new center. At a certified nodal displacement error radius \(e_i\), use the per-coordinate physical bound \(\varepsilon_i=L_0(e_i+\xi_i)\).

The existing exact P2 transfer theorem already proves the \(5\varepsilon_i/4\) coordinate and \(8\varepsilon_i\) radial-derivative bounds. For each stored face \(s\), checked \(m_s=\min R'_s>8\varepsilon_i\), conforming controls, exact fixed axis/rim radii and ROI coverage imply Eulerian height error at most
\[
E_{is}=C_{is}(e_i+\xi_i),\qquad
C_{is}=\tfrac54 L_0\left(1+\max_{\text{edges }a}
   \frac{\max_a|Z'_s|}{\min_a R'_s}\right).
\]
The ratio is taken edgewise before its global maximum. Use the stored graph's slope bound in the height comparison. The positive derivative margin is for the perturbed graph. This formulation remains valid across moving interior edge knots, and does not require the shoulder to stay on the same edge. Prove fixed endpoint radii from the actual free map and reference/binary state. If a replacement mesh leaves either endpoint free, explicitly bound endpoint motion and prove the common current ROI; the axis query also requires special treatment. For a small record the coefficients \(A_{ik}\) of the resulting error are \(C_{ia}+C_{ib}\) for maximum and mean shortening and \(C_{ia},C_{ib}\) for the respective component spatial maxima. This follows from fixed-ROI same-radius height, nonnegative normalized disk weights and supremum stability. Neither differentiating a moving maximizing point nor estimating the 68-observable Jacobian is necessary. One strict design target is
\[
e_i^\mathrm{goal}=\min_k \frac{\sigma m_{ik}}{A_{ik}}-\xi_i,\qquad 0<\sigma<1 .
\]
Use exact fractions, and also require the radial monotonicity/coverage constraints. Reserve the unused margin for explicit uncertainty accounts. Values such as \(\sigma=1/2\) are internal budget choices, with no physical uncertainty implied. Guards should reject a nonpositive target.

For the two large fields use the actual independent signed stored interval, oriented by endpoint label. If it is separated from zero by \(G_s>0\), preserve a predeclared positive fraction of that observed separation. The axis has fixed radial coordinate and its endpoint basis is nodal, so \(Q\) moves by at most
\[
q_j^\mathrm{err}=L_0(e_j+\xi_j)+E_{ja}=(L_0+C_{ja})(e_j+\xi_j).
\]
A balanced design allocates each large field at most \(\sigma G_s/2\), guaranteeing final distance at least \((1-\sigma)G_s\) if the other premises hold. Use the actual \(G_s\), not the planned affine difference near \(0.1549\,\mu\mathrm m\). A signed gap interval must be inflated at both endpoints, even if both forces are extremely small. If working with a desired detection threshold, subtract explicitly bounded measurement, registration and model radii before requiring separation. Nominal imaging spacing is not such an error bound.

## Economical first execution after terminal outcomes

Use the most allowance-limiting small record **for each candidate** and both large cases to pilot the budget and the large geometry. Check exact \(r_i\), a positive input-only geometric ball, and a fresh cached contraction first. Cheap cached feasibility screens identify conditioning/geometry failures before expensive integration. Their success is only an intermediate finite-quadrature result. Start with an independently chosen \(z_*\in(0,1)\) and reserve \(0<\nu<1\). A force budget \(\eta_*= \nu(1-z_*)\min(r_i,e_i^\mathrm{goal})\) and the bound \(z\le z_*\) leave strict self-map/error headroom. Budget local point force truncation by a small fraction of \(\eta_*/(\|R_i\|_\infty c_F)\). Budget uniform local Hessian entry tails by a small fraction of available derivative headroom divided by \(\|R_i\|_\infty c_H\). Here \(c_F\) is maximum assembled force incidence, and \(c_H\) is maximum local-column incidence counted along a free row. Those are truncation budgets only. Full interval widths, cache-site correction, center bias and geometry still decide the proof, as the direct L bound showed. Start at the loosest validated order that respects those caps, retain order attempts, and sharpen only the bottleneck. Where a point residual is genuinely too large, propose a new dyadic center from the signed exact-integral force estimate, rebuild the point enclosure, and verify the transferred observer budget against the old or recomputed center.

Use the previously reviewed mathematical field/moment primitives and corrected scaled physical charts as a fallback for whole-ball Hessian enclosure, but use a new point-context admission layer.
