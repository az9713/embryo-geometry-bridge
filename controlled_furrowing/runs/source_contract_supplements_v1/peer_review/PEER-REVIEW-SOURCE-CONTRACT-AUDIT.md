## Aggregation and geometry remain conditional

Final Sec16/Par59 defines maximum shortening as the largest point/time value in the activation region. Spatial and temporal maxima commute, but the sampled time window and continuous-peak censoring remain specimen-dependent inputs. The same paragraph's literal description of average shortening favors taking each point's temporal maximum and then averaging; it does not justify silently interchanging temporal maximum and spatial averaging. No executable workflow recovered from this peer-review source settles the exact implementation.

Fig2b/b′/b″ shows regional mean surface positions, whereas Fig2e labels separate maximum apical/basal shortenings. This attachment does not unambiguously connect the component endpoint columns to a specified spatial aggregation, temporal operation and growth-reference split. Current disk-weighted static/spatial-maximum model outputs remain expressly declared proxies. Sampling pitch and blinding procedures do not supply an empirical discrepancy law. The5.1/5.2µm queries in the mathematical proof remain conditional illustrations.

The peer review discusses AP/DV curvature and45×15µm ellipses (first rebuttal Reviewer1 point17, pp15–16), but supplies no cohort of specimen-specific initial surfaces with uncertainty bounds. Draft response FigR4 on PDFp27 has0,2,6,10min panel labels but0,2,4,6min in its caption. Final Supplementary Fig9e, PDFp12, retains0,2,6,10min panel labels without that caption time list. This bounded draft-caption inconsistency concerns another cephalic-furrow condition; it cannot establish the calibration cohort's clock.

## Availability and audit scope

All36peer-review pages were read and searchable text plus link inventories retained. The PDF has9URI annotations, all Zotero citation pointers, and no embedded files. No new public raw-microscopy accession or executable measurement workflow was found in this attachment. Final Data availability says microscopy is obtainable by author request; Code availability says no custom code was generated. A useful request therefore seeks exact analysis settings/workflow, individual timecourses, masks/registered surfaces, initial geometry and crosswalks, rather than asserting that the authors withheld declared custom code. No contact was made. This bounded search cannot prove that data are nonexistent or inaccessible elsewhere.

The32–35µm, acquisition-spacing and40s facts mostly reinforce information already present in final Methods and figures. Their significance is correction of this project's earlier overly weak acquisition constraints, not discovery of new biology or a new paper omission. Peer rebuttals add explicit explanations of early deformation, resolution limitations and future reconstruction work. Attachment-specific licensing on PDFp5 differs from the article-level metadata license; no source images are adapted or republished here.
