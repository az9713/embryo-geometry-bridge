# Five-gate status after the first spatial wave

**The original objective remains incomplete.** Eight of the two-candidate study’s 36 fixed-input fields now have independently verified roots and strict local minima for the exactly integrated finite-element model. All 36 already have separate cached-quadrature root and minimum proofs. The original no-refit claim is that small-region calibration can predict observed larger-region deformation. The present exact spatial result separates two **model predictions** at the same prescribed large input. Source-grounded compatibility, propagated identified-set predictions, and a matched experimental comparison remain open. ## Work that can proceed on current inputs

1. **Close the fixed-input finite-element witness.** Under the current per-field protocol, the two frozen candidates need all 17 small cases each to establish all 68 primary inequalities per candidate for the exactly integrated model. Six small cases have four independent checks each, leaving 28 small cases and 112 primary inequalities. The two large contrasts are already certified. Finish the planned signed reductions and root, minimum and observer checks for the remaining explicit cases. Retain incomplete or adverse tests and the original observer/error terms. A theorem that proved the same remaining inequalities in bulk could also close this coverage. Completed cached proofs alone cannot be substituted for the spatial operator.
2. **Compute a conditional region and prediction bound.** After fixed-point coverage, predeclare a local exact-spatial parameter cell, or start with the 34 verified scalar-linked calibration constraints, and propagate a rigorous root/observer remainder with active-maximum switches. Record inner witnesses, branch-specific exclusions and unresolved cells separately. Parameter tails remain explicit. Carry the bound into the same larger-input \(Q\) without using a larger response to choose it. This improves the conditional result while leaving full material/geometry coverage and the source operator open.
3. **Advance numerical adequacy on the same declared model.** A certified continuation over a load interval, a fitted-load discretization/locking and surface-observer error bound, and tests of non-axisymmetric sensitivity address different risks. The discrete endpoint minima do not supply these results. No dynamic comparison is justified solely by a quasistatic continuation.
4. **Freeze comparative tests before a matched score becomes available.** Name eligible inputs, outputs, comparator predictions, allowance handling and decision rules while preventing larger response data from choosing the law, branch, tolerance or scoring functional. A model-class-specific lower bound or measurement budget can be stated conditionally, with published outcomes acknowledged as retrospective context.
