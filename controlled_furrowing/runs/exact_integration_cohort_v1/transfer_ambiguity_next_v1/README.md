## Run and decision

1. Freeze an exact common tolerance parameter \(\delta\) in µm. At each predeclared value, minimize and maximize \(c^Tx\), where \(c=\partial_xQ\) is the large sensitivity, subject to all \(68\) two-sided small constraints and the one-sided step box. For stored intercept intervals \([a_i^-,a_i^+]\) use \(a_i^++J_i x\le\delta\) and \(-a_i^--J_i x\le\delta\). An initial exact grid is \(\{519/100,525/100,538/100,11/2\}\) µm plus the affine minimax floor. This brackets the archived affine floor and baseline and carries no experimental meaning. Preserve infeasible cases with their affine domain. Use the existing minimax checker for the floor. For the signed Q programs retain rounded rational coefficients, primal feasible witnesses, weak-dual bounds or an explicitly floating proposal, all active residual margins, and step stability checks. An exact LP certificate says only what the rounded affine approximation allows. [Existing affine verifier](../../../scripts/bounded_affine_minimax.py#L78)
2. Compare candidate directions at consistent tolerances, using archived Q separation as a computational-investment benchmark. A new tangent plus small LPs and focused graph checks should take tens of seconds plus imports on the prior host, and the entire prior 17-field tangent and observer job took about 87 s. Retain failed checks and stop the local screen if the two Q directions have negligible or unstable separation at comparable small-data residuals. No failure of this local screen exhausts other material regions or shapes.
3. If the screen is promising, freeze both proposals before full solves. Recompute all 17 small doses and the fixed large input for each candidate using the common branch and Q8/Q12 policies. Preserve failed attempts and annotate any warm-start change from zero-dose continuation. Prior fixed-material 17-field replay took about 857 s, and two large pilot cases took 170–189 s each. Budget roughly 35 sequential minutes for a two-candidate first replay before rigorous root validation. Check force, actual installed parameters, doses, positive-J and embedding, radial graph and thickness guards. Retain exact 68-row stored-field residual intervals and signed Q intervals.
4. For each pair, report the upper endpoint of its largest absolute small residual as the common tolerance breakpoint. This gives sufficiency for the stored curves under the stated convention. Compare signed Q gaps across the frozen tolerance grid, preserving overlaps and threshold-straddling cases. The set of tried pairs supplies attainable widths within the sample. Validated upper bounds on all possible response widths would require full coverage including branches and tails.

A finite-model witness requires independently bounded equilibrium positions and 68 observations for both candidates, one common sufficient \(\delta\), and nonoverlapping equilibrium Q intervals. Source-comparable robust ambiguity further requires one common admitted uncertainty and observation contract for all records and the large profile. The public source does not establish a measured tolerance, the specimen/acquisition crosswalk, matched registered boundaries and moving ROIs, temporal counterfactual reference curves and component allocations, per-record windows, larger-section time and plane, or the required uncertainty bounds. Preserve outcome withholding throughout discovery. A very narrow stored-curve interpolation interval, a force residual, or a certified root at another candidate cannot close these gaps.
