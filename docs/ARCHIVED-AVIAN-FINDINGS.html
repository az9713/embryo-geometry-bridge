Findings, significance, and completion boundary

Assessment: a verified component study and a completed negative baseline audit; the full 15-step biological project is not complete. There is no confirmed new scientific concept, molecular-to-force calibration, causal mechanism discrimination, or validated 3D folding forecast. Procedural checks, predictive performance, and originality are separate judgments.

What was actually learned

Latest geometric forecasting result: the locked planar stress test uses only the first two frames to predict later extracted mask proxies in one attached and one detached example. It fails its uniform affine-versus-persistence criterion. Detached centred-overlap gains are negative in all 27 declared initial/target threshold comparisons; exact conditional raw-mask gain bounds are also negative at all three detached horizons. Some attached point scores improve, but not robustly across the declared threshold family. These are retrospective proxy forecasts, not independently withheld-perturbation mechanochemical validation. A known initial segmentation failure and a source-law difference between intact/cut implementations remain explicit. The executable comparison is complete; the successful biological prediction milestone is not established.

  1. Published mechanochemical research already bridges biology and continuum mechanics. The originality audit found that mechanical feedback, tissue regulation, instability calculations, geometry control and morphogen transport are established research. Our reference implementations reproduce or inspect those structures; they do not originate them. See the audit and final-supplement comparison.
  2. The source data support a useful conditional prediction test. Four supplementary workbook sheets contain cohort measurements, in addition to the 29-TIFF image package. One sheet supplies 720 position/deformation/GDF1 rows from 38 identified embryos. Training and evaluation keep entire embryos or conditions together. A decreasing isotonic response to measured endpoint deformation has embryo-averaged RMSE 0.18513 against 0.39493 for a training-mean baseline under leave-one-embryo-out evaluation; under leave-one-condition-out evaluation, the corresponding values are 0.19007 and 0.40618. Weighted linear regression is competitive at 0.19521 and 0.19605. These are dimensionless normalized-reporter errors, not force errors. There is no claimed significant superiority of isotonic over linear regression. See protocol, all features, and limitations.
  3. That response test is not an initial-state forecast. Its input is measured accumulated deformation at the endpoint. It cannot show that a molecular model predicts later mechanics, distinguish causal feedback direction, or explain the origin of the body axis. The second feature reconstruction uses different source stage normalizations for controls and halves. Performance worsens across conditions: raw-log-length isotonic RMSE is 0.21540. This sensitivity is retained. Published outcomes were already known; no prospective or externally preregistered validation is claimed. Version 1 lacked a prediction-file-before-score receipt; version 2 fixes that workflow, reproduces all twelve scores, and preserves version 1.
  4. Exact baseline reproduction is not established. The intact eight-hour simulation runs in DOLFIN 2019.2 and an isolated exact 2019.1 environment. Both fail the original strict serialized-reference criterion (relative tolerance 1e-4, absolute 1e-8); no threshold was relaxed. The original cut simulation repeatedly halves margin resolution during remeshing and crashes. A separately labeled minimal repair completes four hours. A supplied-initial-mesh probe matches all six initial fields exactly, while independently generated initial meshes differ. This localizes one discrepancy but does not reconcile the full dynamics. See the numerical comparisons and negative receipt.
  5. Several mathematical and numerical components are independently verified. Exact rational brackets certify linear growth-rate signs in an explicitly dimensionless margin example. A separate published Serra equilibrium/spectrum result is reproduced. Manufactured Stokes solutions converge using both the source finite-element form and an independent staggered-grid implementation. Material maps agree with affine matrix-exponential solutions. The source's periodically resampled contour indices are not persistent tissue labels. These checks support implementation correctness within their stated domains, not validation of avian constitutive laws.
  6. Flow alone need not identify absolute force. A common rescaling of pressure, viscosity, drag and active stress leaves compatible homogeneous mechanical equations unchanged. Independently calibrated force or material measurements are necessary to remove that ambiguity. Along a specified algebraic stress/strain relation, unrestricted feedback functions can also be reparameterized. This does not prove that every restricted competing mechanism is indistinguishable. These are conditional applications of established inverse-problem reasoning. See the proof.
  7. Public 3D geometry is usable but not interchangeable with validated shell mechanics. Six supplied chick cranial surfaces were analyzed. Three fail closure/orientation checks; enclosed-volume interpretations are withheld. One purported lumen collection file exactly equals a supplied basal surface. The CSV contains seven HH17 and six HH20 IDs, unlike the caption's seven each. Available-record area and volume ratios are 4.35548 and 9.42073, calculated separately from mesh values. Geometry invariance, sphere convergence and independent-library checks pass. Self-intersection, segmentation-error, anatomical labeling and dynamic shell validation remain unresolved. This is adjacent later chick anatomy, not early-quail dynamic validation. See the complete 3D audit.

How the chemistry–biology–physics–geometry bridge should be judged

The chain is molecular state → active stress/material response → force balance → tissue motion → material deformation → geometry. Each arrow requires an observation operator, independently constrained parameters and an intervention that distinguishes plausible alternatives. Reporter intensity is not motor activity, and neither is an absolute force measurement. A moving planar contour is not a three-dimensional embryo.

The present study verifies pieces of the calculation chain and identifies data and inference constraints. It has not established a new bridge across those disciplines. Existing publications already provide substantive biological bridges; our contribution so far is an inspectable reproduction/inference audit with a narrowly scoped retrospective response analysis. Nothing here resolves philosophy or proves a general explanation of biological form.

What remains before the project can be called complete

  • Reconcile the baseline: establish which exact archived initialization, parameter discretization and execution produced the reference arrays. Preserve the strict failed comparison. An explicitly revised tolerance requires an independent justification and a new evaluation version, not retrospective acceptance of the current output.
  • Validate observations on biology: independently annotated landmarks and streak masks; paired reporter/flow registration; derivative and out-of-plane uncertainty; documented biological time origin. The current synthetic recovery checks and smoothing sensitivities do not supply these.
  • Constrain the closures: independently calibrated activity/force/material scales or an explicitly identifiable reduced parameterization; compare restricted dynamic feedback laws under shared information and noise. The endpoint workbook alone does not measure the missing time-dependent quantities.
  • Complete the dynamic test: lock initial-state and intervention inputs, fit on training embryos, save joint molecular/flow/material-map/streak predictions, then score withheld embryos or treatments without retuning. The current conditional endpoint test cannot substitute for this.
  • Control mathematical uncertainty: establish the regularity and error constants actually needed for a coupled nonlinear trajectory/strain guarantee. Two-level mesh sensitivity is evidence, not a rigorous numerical enclosure.
  • Pass the planar gates before 3D: secure appropriately matched surface/thickness/force observations, resolve source labels and mesh defects, verify active-shell limits and score a withheld fold. Do not prescribe the desired shape as input.

No author was contacted and no new experiment was run. Missing independent biological information cannot be manufactured by more code or synthetic examples. The unresolved full gates remain BLOCKED or IN PROGRESS in the status ledger, rather than being relabeled PASS.

Strongest next scientific target

Ask a narrower question: which intervention and measured variable make a specified pair of dynamic feedback laws distinguishable after force-scale ambiguity and observation noise are accounted for? First audit the exact proposed discrimination criterion against inverse mechanics and experimental-design literature. Then use source-consistent synthetic recovery to identify information requirements, followed by retrospective paired biological evaluation if the necessary records exist. A new, proven separation criterion with experimentally attainable measurements would be more substantive than another shape simulation. Its novelty is currently unestablished.
